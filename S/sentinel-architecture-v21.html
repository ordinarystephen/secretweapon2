A governed execution platform for credit review

Sentinel moves the reviewer's starting point from assembling information to adjudicating a prepared, evidence-backed credit assessment: assemble, assess, challenge, adjudicate. The architecture separates deterministic authority from bounded probabilistic reasoning, and makes the audit trail a property of the topology rather than a feature.

The LLM may propose facts, interpretations, plans, and challenges. It never determines authority, access, workflow state, evidence identity, or final judgment.

Data model — working draftillustrative, not definitive

A working example of the shapes the guarantees require — enough to pin down what traceability, lineage, provenance, and auditability mean, and deliberately nothing more. It is organized by ownership: the semantic model (what things mean) is CRR's as data owner; the physical model (how things are stored) belongs to the platform teams and is left open here — including whether existing platform stores are adopted as-is, enhanced, or bridged by an interim solution. The middle tier is the contract between the two: CRR states the requirement, the platform chooses the mechanism, and conformance is the acceptance test.

How the model scales — platform core, tenant extensions

Two layers with different change regimes. The core is invariant substrate no tenant configures, because it is what auditability is. Everything above it is declared per application, as configuration.

Anticipated questions & blockers

The challenges we expect, raised before they are raised. Four statuses: position ready — the trade-off was weighed and the answer stands; to negotiate — a genuine trade with room to move; owed — a real gap, work in progress; deliberately open — not ours to answer, owner named. A question that lands in already-routed territory is the process working, not a hole in it.
