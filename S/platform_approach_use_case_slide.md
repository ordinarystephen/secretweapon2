## Sentinel GRC Accelerator | What each business area gets, by delivery phase

> Each phase builds on one shared foundation: documents processed once, reused by every business area.

> **Note:** 2027 deliverables need validation against proposed IT plans. Work in progress.

| Business area | As of Today<br><sub>End-user functionality in the existing Risk Lab prototypes</sub> | Strategic Sentinel MVP \| Q1-27<br><sub>Migration from local drives to the strategic platform; uploaded documents can be reused</sub> | Last Phase \| Q4-27<br><sub>Advanced capabilities and scalability</sub> |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | • Risk Lab prototypes run from local drives, with no common interface.<br>• Each prototype keeps its own copies of documents; nothing is shared across teams. | • Prototypes move to the strategic platform with one common interface.<br>• Documents are processed once and stored centrally, reusable by any authorized area.<br>• Every answer keeps its source: document, page and quoted text.<br>• Access controls, audit logging and a record of human decisions built in from day one. | • Business areas configure their own templates, question sets and policies without new code.<br>• New business areas onboard through configuration, not a rebuild.<br>• Each new application inherits the documents and knowledge already on the platform. |
| **IB Commercial Lending**<br><sub>End-user capabilities</sub> | • Credit officers can upload annual and quarterly review credit memos and ask natural-language questions to identify counterparty exposures and risk events.<br>• Uploaded documents are not stored or able to be re-used. | • The standalone application moves onto the shared platform.<br>• Credit officers ask which clients are exposed to an event and get answers across all stored documents.<br>• Each answer shows the quote and page behind it; unsupported answers are flagged, not guessed.<br>• Coverage extends beyond credit memos to *[doc types to confirm]*. | • Saved event questions rerun as new documents arrive.<br>• Results viewable by client or by question, with export for reporting.<br>• Searches can draw on documents other areas have already processed, subject to access rights. |
| **CRR CUSO**<br><sub>End-user capabilities</sub> | • Reviewers upload a borrower's credit documents and ask questions or run a standard question set.<br>• The prototype drafts workpaper content, citing the page each answer came from.<br>• Documents are not stored or reused between reviews. | • Reviewers start a review by uploading documents or selecting ones already on the platform.<br>• Sentinel drafts the six-section workpaper from CRR's standard review questions, every item cited to document and page.<br>• Items needing attention are flagged; reviewers edit or clear content, with their rationale recorded.<br>• The workpaper exports to Word in the standard CRR format. | • Assessments against CRR policies and standards, with insufficient evidence flagged explicitly.<br>• A challenge view presents a counter-position, even when no issue is found.<br>• Repeat reviews show what changed since the last review of the same borrower.<br>• Reviewer rulings form an auditable record that informs future reviews through controlled configuration. |
| **P&C Vantage**<br><sub>End-user capabilities</sub> | • Users upload a set of documents and ask the same questions of each; answers return as a table with the source page.<br>• Already used for portfolio scans, including the production-facilities and Swiss watch supplier analyses.<br>• Documents are not stored or reused. | • Users upload documents or spreadsheets (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV) and ask in plain language.<br>• Clicking any value shows it highlighted on the source page or row.<br>• Tracking sheets are searchable by meaning, including entries in French, Italian and German.<br>• A client's full document set can be scanned for a specific mention, such as a key customer. | • Saved extraction templates for recurring scans, set up by the business.<br>• Results hand off to connected systems for follow-on checks, such as the rating database.<br>• Extraction offered as a service other platforms can call. |
