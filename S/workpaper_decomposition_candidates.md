# Linecard Decomposition v2 — Candidate Questions for SME Review

Drafted 2026-08-20 from a credit-review critique of the leveraged lending decomposition (see `claude/linecard-decomposition-leveraged-lending.md`). Steve agreed with all six themes. These are **proposals for the SME to react to** — adopt, adapt, or reject; they are not procedure. Each adopted question also becomes a Sentinel completeness-check / rubric candidate.

## Theme 1 — Liquidity & runway (add to Financials)
- What is the borrower's 12–18 month liquidity runway (cash plus revolver availability against fixed charges, capex, and working capital needs) under the UBS Downside Case?
- What is current revolver availability, and does drawing it spring any covenant test?
- What debt maturities fall due within the next 24 months, and what is the refinancing plan and market access?
- What share of floating-rate debt is hedged? When do hedges/caps expire, and what is the unhedged interest-expense sensitivity?

## Theme 2 — Downside symmetry (extend Financials / EV coverage)
- Does the **UBS Downside Case** EV cover Total Debt? If not, where in the capital structure does value break, and what does that imply for LGD and expected recovery?
- Under the Downside Case, does the borrower still delever / retain repayment capacity over the projection period?
- (Retain the existing Expected Case coverage question; the downside counterpart is the more probative test.)

## Theme 3 — Covenant trajectory & documentation capacity (Underwriting & Documentation / Portfolio Management)
- What is the current headroom (%) against the tightest financial covenant, and its projected trajectory over the next four quarters?
- Is the facility cov-lite or maintenance? Which tests apply, and when?
- How broad is the contractual EBITDA definition — are addbacks capped? Which uncapped addback categories exist?
- What restricted payment, restricted investment, and incremental facility capacity exists, and could it materially increase leverage or leak value from the credit?

## Theme 4 — Findings-to-ratings reconciliation (add to Risk Rating Recognition — CRR Assessment)
- Are the weaknesses identified in the Financials and Underwriting & Documentation sections reflected in the regulatory, PD, and LGD ratings? Where a rating is unchanged despite identified weaknesses, is the rationale documented?
- Are CRR's section-level assessments internally consistent with the final rating conclusion and any proposed Grading Difference?
- (Rationale: operationalizes the documentation chain's "conclusion follows from documented analysis" requirement; the most common workpaper deficiency. Cheap to add, high value.)

## Theme 5 — Data recency (Borrower/Counterparty Overview + general)
- What is the as-of date of the financial data used (statement date vs. review date)? Are more recent interim results or covenant compliance certificates available?
- Have data points carried forward from the prior review been re-verified against source, rather than copied?
- (Sentinel implication: pre-populated figures must carry as-of dates and a re-verification flag — copying from the prior review is where stale data propagates, and automation amplifies it.)

## Theme 6 — Sponsor conduct (Borrower/Counterparty Overview)
- What is the sponsor's track record on this and comparable credits — dividend recaps, support provided in prior stress, liability-management/LMT history?
- What is the sponsor's remaining equity cushion at the current implied valuation, and does it still incentivize support?
- (Rationale: the decomposition already tests J.Crew/Serta/Chewy protections; sponsor conduct is the risk those protections exist for.)
